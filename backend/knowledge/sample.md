Harbor Desk is the help assistant for Northwind Outfitters. It answers questions from company documents that have been stored in the vector database.

Northwind Outfitters sells outdoor clothing and camping gear. The flagship store is at 18 Marine Drive, Kochi. Store hours are Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 20:00 IST. The store is closed on Sunday.

Free shipping applies to orders of 75 USD or more inside India. Orders below that amount have a flat shipping fee of 6 USD. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days.

Customers can return unworn items within 30 days if they still have the receipt. Refunds go back to the original payment method. The Coral loyalty tier starts at 500 points. Members of Coral receive free shipping on every order and a birthday coupon for 10 USD.

Support for online orders is available on weekdays from 09:00 to 17:00 IST. The support desk does not handle wholesale accounts. Wholesale buyers should email trade@northwind.example.

The Harbor Desk chatbot only uses passages retrieved from the document collection. If a question is not covered by those passages, the assistant should say it does not know.
